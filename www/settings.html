About Reverse Flow Fire hydraulics support and app information.

What is Reverse Flow?

Reverse Flow is a fire hydraulics calculator and reference platform designed for firefighters, pump operators, company officers, and instructors.

It combines operational hydraulic calculators, department reference tools, pump chart management, and training resources into a single application.

Calculator Modes

Reverse Flow includes Reverse Flow, Required PDP, Apparatus Mounted, Relay Pumping, and Split Lay modes for common fireground hydraulic scenarios.

Pump Charts

Pump Charts allow departments and individual users to save, manage, update, and export commonly used operational hose and nozzle configurations.

Tools & References (Pro)

The Tools workspace provides additional hydraulic calculators, hose management, department defaults, reference materials, and training resources.

Disclaimer

Reverse Flow is intended for training, planning, reference, and operational decision support. Always follow department policy, manufacturer guidance, verified pump charts, and instructor direction.
