Reverse Flow is a fire service hydraulic calculator and utility application.

Information Collection

Reverse Flow does not require personal information and does not sell personal information.

App Analytics and Attribution

Reverse Flow uses Meta (Facebook) App Events for app analytics and advertising attribution. Meta App Events may collect information such as app launches, app interactions, verified Pro purchases, and basic device and app information needed for attribution. Reverse Flow does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not collect Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). Meta App Events are used to measure advertising effectiveness and improve the app.

User Accounts

Reverse Flow does not require user accounts or login credentials.

Location Data

Reverse Flow does not collect location data.

Advertising

Reverse Flow does not display third-party advertisements, does not collect IDFA, and does not use Facebook Login, Facebook Sharing, or Audience Network features.

Data Sharing

Reverse Flow shares limited app event information with Meta for analytics and advertising attribution as described above. Reverse Flow does not sell user data.

Children's Privacy

Reverse Flow is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.
