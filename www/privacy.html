Introduction

Reverse Flow is a fire service hydraulic calculator and utility application. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use Reverse Flow.

Information We Collect

Reverse Flow does not require user accounts or login credentials. The app stores calculator settings, saved calculations, and the last confirmed Supporter state on the device.

Apple or Google provides purchase and subscription status directly to the app for billing controls. That store status is not used by the Reverse Flow backend to decide Supporter identity. A name and email address are collected only when you voluntarily join or recover Supporter status. The app may also produce anonymous operational diagnostics needed to investigate failures and improve reliability; these diagnostics exclude full email addresses, receipts, purchase tokens, transaction identifiers, and calculator content.

Calculator inputs and fireground calculations remain on your device. Reverse Flow does not transmit or upload them.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store on iOS devices and Google Play on Android devices. The current store account determines purchase availability, active monthly support, restoration, and subscription changes. Reverse Flow does not use its backend as a second billing authority.

Reverse Flow does not receive or store complete payment-card information for those purchases.

Supporter Registration

Joining the Supporter Community is voluntary. Reverse Flow asks for a name and email address when a user joins or recovers Supporter status. The Reverse Flow backend uses the normalized email address to maintain permanent, provider-neutral Supporter identity and prevent duplicate registrations. Before submission, the app discloses that submitting the form adds the provided name to the public Reverse Flow Supporter Registry. The user affirmatively joins by submitting that form; no separate public-listing checkbox is required. A Supporter may request removal from the public registry at any time.

Supporter identity and public recognition are maintained independently from Apple, Google, or Stripe billing state. A purchase does not automatically create, prioritize, or guarantee Supporter recognition. Public Supporter listings display only the chosen public name and Supporting Since date. Email addresses, purchase history, provider account information, and billing details are never displayed publicly.

Third-Party Services

The mobile applications use Apple App Store and Google Play Billing for purchases, Supabase for protected backend infrastructure and Supporter records, and Resend for Supporter welcome emails. These services receive only the information needed for their stated role.

How We Use Information

Reverse Flow uses voluntarily submitted information to maintain Supporter status, deliver the welcome email, recover Supporter status across devices, and prevent duplicate records. Apple and Google provide billing state directly to the app.

Data Sharing

Apple or Google processes store purchases. Reverse Flow uses Supabase and Resend to store Supporter records and deliver welcome emails. Reverse Flow does not sell personal information.

Website and Mobile Applications

The Reverse Flow website may use analytics technologies such as the Meta Pixel to understand website traffic and improve the website experience. The native Reverse Flow mobile applications do not include the Meta SDK and do not transmit mobile usage events to Meta.

Information We Do Not Collect

Reverse Flow does not collect precise location, contacts, health or fitness data, photos or videos, text messages, browsing history, search history, payment card details, advertising identifiers, or sensitive information.

User Choices

You may join without purchasing, purchase without joining, do both, or do neither. You can continue using every calculator without purchasing or joining the Supporter Community. To request removal from the public registry, contact support@reverse-flow.app. Transaction records may be retained where needed for accounting, fraud prevention, platform reconciliation, legal compliance, or dispute handling even when a public listing is removed.

For privacy questions, contact Reverse Flow support at support@reverse-flow.app.

Children's Privacy

Reverse Flow is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Data Security

Reverse Flow uses reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app and by service providers used for purchase verification, Supporter registration, and email delivery.

Changes to This Policy

Reverse Flow may update this policy when app features or privacy practices change. The effective date above shows when this policy was last updated.
